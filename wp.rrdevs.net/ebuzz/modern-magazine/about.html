5+ Years in Porto

Olá and welcome to PortoExpats.pt! 👋

My name is Gabriela, and like many of you, I fell in love with Porto – a city that has truly captured my heart. I’m originally from Slovakia, and my journey with Porto began on May 30, 2013. Some say 13 is an unlucky number, but for me, it marked the beginning of something beautiful. It was the year I landed at Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport for the first time – to visit someone special I met during Erasmus. That love story continues to this day… and so does my love for Porto.

“Since then, I’ve lived in Porto (with a few breaks) for over 5 years. I got married here and slowly gathered a wealth of knowledge – not just about the city, but about its food, people, language, traditions, local products, and how to truly enjoy life here.”

People often ask me: “Do you feel more like an expat or a local?” Honestly, I feel a bit (or rather a lot 😊) of both. And that’s exactly why I created this space – to connect expats with locals, and help both communities learn from, respect and support one another.
